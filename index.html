Monthly Financial Update Tool

A web tool for a chemicals manufacturer that turns each month's general-ledger export into their updated P&L statement and FIFO stock-costing workbooks.

ProblemEvery month the finance team copied a new trial balance into two complex Excel workbooks by hand, where one wrong row breaks the totals.
Built
  • Upload once; rows are matched by GL code, new accounts are inserted in place and the new month is added as a column
  • Formulas for subtotals, year-to-date figures and the FIFO stock roll-forward carry forward automatically
  • A review page shows where every value came from before anything is saved; nothing is guessed
  • Automatic backups, one-click undo, full history and a "replace latest month" option for corrected data
Month-end P&L and stock reporting in one upload, with a review step and undo
