Monthly Financial Update Tool

A web tool for a chemicals manufacturer. It turns each month's general-ledger export into their updated P&L statement and FIFO stock-costing workbooks, work the finance team used to copy in by hand.

  1. Upload the month's GL export once
  2. Rows are matched by GL code; new accounts are inserted in place
  3. The new month is added as a column and formulas carry forward
  4. Review every value before saving, with backups and one-click undo
